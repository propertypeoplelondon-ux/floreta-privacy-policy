Floreta - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Floreta (package name com.floreta.garden) is a language-learning app that helps you grow a "memory garden" of words through spaced repetition, listening, speaking and picture-based exercises. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

  • Floreta has no user accounts. We do not know who you are.
  • Your learning progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Some features send the text you type or say, or a photo you choose, to Google to be translated, spoken aloud or read. This content is processed to deliver the feature and is not stored by us or linked to you.

Information stored on your device

To work as a learning app, Floreta keeps the following in its private app storage on your phone: your chosen languages and level, the words you have planted, review history and scheduling data, streaks, settings, and daily usage counters for the free image-translation limit. This data never leaves the device except through Android's standard app backup, which you control in your phone's settings. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Information processed to provide features

FeatureWhat is processedWhereStored?
Speaking exercises Your voice while you hold the microphone button Android's built-in speech recogniser on your device (Google or your phone maker's speech service, depending on your phone) Not by Floreta. Only the recognised text is kept, to grade the exercise.
Pronunciation playback The word or sentence to be spoken Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, or your device's offline text-to-speech No
Text and conversation translation The text you type or dictate Google Cloud Translation, or Google ML Kit on your device when an offline language pack is downloaded No
Image translation The photo you take or choose Text is recognised on your device (Google ML Kit); the recognised text is then translated as above. The photo itself is not uploaded. No
Add Word (dictionary lookup) The English word you look up dictionaryapi.dev and Wiktionary (Wikimedia Foundation) No

Google processes this content under the Google Cloud Privacy Notice. Floreta sends only the content needed for the request; it does not send your name, email, contacts, location or any advertising identifier, because it does not have them.

Permissions

  • Microphone - speaking exercises and voice input in the translator. Used only while you press the microphone button.
  • Camera - taking a photo for image translation. Optional; you can also pick an existing picture.
  • Notifications - optional study reminders at a time you choose.
  • Internet - online translation, cloud voices and dictionary lookup.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android settings; the related feature stops working and everything else continues.

Children

Floreta is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices and data deletion

Because all of your data lives on your device, you delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Floreta. There is no server-side copy for us to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will help.
